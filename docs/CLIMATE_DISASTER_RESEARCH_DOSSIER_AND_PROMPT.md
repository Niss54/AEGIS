### Case 1: Nepal Late September 2024–2026 Bagmati Cloudburst Catastrophe
* **Date:** September 27–29, 2024 (with compounding monsoon aftermath into 2025/2026).
* **Location:** Kathmandu Valley, Lalitpur, Kavrepalanchok, and the Bagmati & Nakkhu River basins, Nepal.
* **Empirical Scale:**
  - **Precipitation:** Record torrential rainfall exceeding **240 mm to 322 mm in 48 hours**, representing the highest recorded rainfall in Kathmandu meteorological stations since 1970.
  - **Human Toll:** **Over 230 verified deaths**, with dozens missing and thousands displaced onto rooftops.
  - **Economic Damage:** Initial government economic loss assessment exceeded **17 Billion Nepalese Rupees (~$127 Million USD)**, disabling major highway arteries, washing away bridges, and crippling national hydropower generation capacity.
* **Core Problem Statement Failures:**
  1. *Early Warning Latency:* National weather models tracked incoming rain, but there was a catastrophic failure in converting precipitation radar into ward-level inundation warnings for informal settlements along the Bagmati floodplains.
  2. *Relief Distribution Bottleneck:* Survivors experienced critical multi-week delays in receiving emergency drinking water, temporary shelter, and compensation due to manual paperwork and impassable physical corridors.
  3. *Uninsured Protection Gap:* Hydropower facilities and smallholder properties suffered near-total unhedged financial ruin due to the absence of parametric disaster bonds.
* **Authoritative Institutional Sources:**

  - *International Centre for Integrated Mountain Development (ICIMOD) Disaster Assessment:* [icimod.org](https://www.icimod.org)
